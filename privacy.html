Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Overview

Remote Spots ("we", "our", "the Service") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

The short version: We collect minimal data. We don't track your searches, store your locations, or sell any information to third parties.

2. Data We Collect

Analytics Data (Vercel Analytics)

We use Vercel Analytics to understand general usage patterns. This includes:

  • Page views and general traffic data
  • Referrer information (how you found us)
  • Country-level geographic data
  • Device type and browser information

Vercel Analytics is privacy-focused and does not use cookies or track individual users across sessions.

Payment Data

If you purchase the Pro plan, payment is processed by Lemon Squeezy. We do not store your credit card information. Lemon Squeezy may collect your email address and billing information as required to process the transaction. Please refer to Lemon Squeezy's Privacy Policy for details.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your search queries or coordinates
  • Your location history
  • Personal identification information (unless you purchase Pro)
  • Cookies for tracking purposes

4. OpenStreetMap Queries

When you perform a search, queries are sent to the Overpass API (OpenStreetMap infrastructure) to retrieve road and geographic data. These queries contain the geographic area you selected but are not linked to any personal identifier. We do not log or store these queries.

5. Local Storage

The app may use your browser's local storage to save preferences (such as your Pro access status). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

6. Third-Party Services

The Service uses the following third-party services:

  • Vercel – Hosting and analytics
  • Lemon Squeezy – Payment processing
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass API – Geographic data
  • MapTiler – Map tiles

Each service has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

7. Data Retention

We do not retain personal data beyond what is necessary for the Service to function. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized. Payment records are retained by Lemon Squeezy as required for transaction processing and tax compliance.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it. Since we collect minimal personal data, most users have no stored personal information with us. If you have questions or requests, contact us at support@remote-spots.com.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@remote-spots.com.
